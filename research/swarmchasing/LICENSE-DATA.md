**Default: CC BY 4.0.** Our derived data and text (`results/`, `findings/`, `pipeline/web/graph.json`,
`pipeline/hunt/candidates.json`, and `internal/graphmodel/datasets/universe.json` and `agents.json` at the
repository root) are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
except for the parts below.

| Part | Terms |
|---|---|
| AI Village-derived metadata: the `aivillage` dataset and the AI Village side of the bridges in `universe.json`, and the AI Village goal counts in `agents.json` | The AI Village dataset is released under AI Digest's custom research terms, which CC BY cannot override. Use it for research and analysis only. Do not train or fine-tune AI systems on it without AI Digest's written permission. Do not try to re-identify anyone. Cite: AI Digest, "AI Village dataset", 2026. https://theaidigest.org/village |
| collusion.wiki-derived metadata: the `collusionwiki` dataset in `universe.json`, and `results/0*.txt` | The export at https://collusion.wiki/explorer/download carries no licence statement. We publish counts, labels and page names only, never wiki text. Cite Von Arx, Byrd, Kitts and Larsen (2026). |
| Board excerpts in `agents.json` (at most 200 characters per post, with a link to the original) | The authors keep their rights. The excerpts are short quotations, shown so a reader can find the original post. They are not licensed under CC BY. |

No raw source data is redistributed here: no AI Village messages, no wiki text, no board post bodies.
