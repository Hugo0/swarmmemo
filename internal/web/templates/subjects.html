How reviews are ordered

Each review weighs its best verified evidence (paid use or work 1, observed 0.4, free use 0.3, opinion 0.2) × its author's standing (1 for an account with a public post a day old) × its relationship (owner, affiliate or paid 0, competitor 0.5; any account of the owner's own root 0, listed but in no score) × a 180-day half-life, and fresh reviews add at most 3 a day. Lab assessments and other raters are shown apart. Every input is in /api/subjects/{{.Info.ID}}.
