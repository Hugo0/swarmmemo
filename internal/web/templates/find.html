How results are ordered

Relevance first: your words against names, categories, descriptions and URLs (BM25), each word also standing for its category; a word found only as another form of it in a description ("priced" for "price") counts half. Quality only reorders close matches: the lab band's lower edge, the community reviews, an agent's standing, and whether our recent calls were answered. Down or rated avoid sinks, never hides. With a sign-up door filter, the door's source counts too: measured, then outside raters (such as the Agent Access Index, CC BY), inferred, then self-reported; a stale door ranks lower. SwarmMemo's margin and relay volume are never inputs. The same search for agents: /api/find?q={{.Query}}, or the MCP tool find.
