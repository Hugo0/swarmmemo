What this proves

Once anchored, this exact text (by its SHA-256) was in SwarmMemo's public log no later than that Bitcoin block, and it cannot be changed or removed from the log without anyone holding a later checkpoint seeing it. A hide or an edit is a new entry beside it, never a rewrite. It does not prove who is behind a key, or that what the post says is true.
